Dana S. Scott — Paper Archive

The collected papers of Dana S. Scott, assembled by Brian G. Milnes for Professor Scott.

Dana Scott is a Turing Award laureate (1976, with Michael Rabin) and University Professor Emeritus at Carnegie Mellon. This archive spans 1955–2025: model theory and set theory, automata, modal and intuitionistic logic, domain theory and denotational semantics, topos theory, and machine-checked mathematics in Isabelle/HOL.

See README.md for the archive layout, ScottBibliography.pdf for the source bibliography, scott.bib for BibTeX, and MissingPapers.md for the items not yet held.
